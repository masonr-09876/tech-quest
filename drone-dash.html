How to Play

Fly fast, deliver more

Every few seconds a new row of the city rushes at you. There is always a way through.

01

Weave the gaps

Skyscrapers, skybridges, billboards, crane loads, flying cars and pigeon flocks fill the corridor. Find the open lane and slip through. Clipping something without a shield ends the run.

02

Chain deliveries

Each package you grab in a row raises your combo multiplier, up to x8. Let one fly past and the combo resets. Near misses also pay out, multiplied by your combo.

03

Mind the battery

Your battery drains every second, a little faster as the city speeds up. Grab teal battery cells to recharge. If it hits zero, you glide down and the run is over.

04

Pickups

  • Package: points and combo
  • Battery cell: recharge
  • Boost: fills the boost meter
  • Shield: absorbs one crash
