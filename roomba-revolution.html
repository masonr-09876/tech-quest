🎮 The Core Gameplay Loop

Suck, Aim & Escape

  • 1. Suck: Cruise over debris while holding Suck Mode to fill your "Inventory Belly." Different items have different weight and properties.
  • 2. Aim & Launch: Use your directional laser sight to blast items out of your exhaust hatch and trigger chain reactions.
  • 3. Recharge: Keep an eye on your battery! Sneak onto glowing green charging docks around the room to recharge before you power down.

🧹 Your Absurd Ammo Types

Weaponize the Mess

  • Marbles / Lego Bricks: Highly bouncy. Shooting these creates a "slip zone" that makes pursuing humans comically wipe out.
  • Stray Socks: Clogs up mechanical hazards like laser sensors and spinning blades, shutting them down temporarily.
  • Pet Food / Catnip: Distracts territorial cats—or shoot it near a human so the cat attacks their ankles!
  • Soda Cans: High-pressure rocket projectiles that smash open wooden cabinets, fragile vents, and heavy obstacles.

🏡 High-Action Levels

3 Stages to Conquer

  • Stage 1 — The Kitchen Disaster: Dodge a cereal-throwing toddler. Shoot the fridge target or noisy blender to knock loose the exit key.
  • Stage 2 — The Living Room War Zone: A lazy Golden Retriever blocks the exit couch. Blast squeaky toys or pet food across the room to lure him away.
  • Stage 3 — The Backyard Jungle: Tall grass drains your battery fast. Outrun the spinning automated lawnmower—your ultimate rival!

⚡ Fun Upgrade System

The "Mod" Station

  • All-Terrain Monster Wheels: Roll over thick carpets and muddy grass without slowing down or drifting on hardwood.
  • The Turbo-Suck 9000: Increases your vacuum vortex radius, letting you pull in ammo and Dust Bunnies from further away.
  • Nitrous Boost Exhaust: Fire a blast of air backward for a sudden speed burst to dodge human foot stomps.
